## New Features

- Merchants with an Australian company can now settle and get paid out in AUD on NEXT Payments, to avoid FX conversion.
- A rebuilt [Campaigns Cart SDK](https://developers.nextcommerce.com/docs/campaigns) is now available in beta, loading a fraction of the JavaScript and skipping the debugger outside debug mode, so prices show faster.
- Introducing the `subscription.upcoming_renewal` webhook event, sent 72 hours before a subscription renews to help act on renewals ahead of time.

## Improvements

- Login, NEXT Payments and Partners pages in your NEXT account have been redesigned for an improved user experience.
- Campaigns API orders now pass marketing URL parameters to `success_url` and `payment_failed_url`, so tracking pixels fire after every payment.
- Storefront checkout now uses the local address format for customers in Arab countries, to help increase sales in the Middle East.
- Checkout hero images can now be up to twice as large, to better reflect your brand at checkout.
- Transactions on the [Admin API](https://developers.nextcommerce.com/docs/admin-api) and transaction webhooks now include AVS and CVV results for your fraud and chargeback services.
- Klarna notification disputes now come in as RFIs, with guidance on resolving them with the customer before they become a dispute.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where [Shop Sync](https://docs.nextcommerce.com/docs/apps/shop-sync) marked authorized payments as paid in Shopify, letting them be refunded there before capture.
- Fixed an issue where Shop Sync setup asked for a Shopify webhook version that Shopify has retired, which stalled new installs.
