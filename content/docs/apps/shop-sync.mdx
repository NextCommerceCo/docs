---
title: "Shop Sync"
description: "Sync Shopify products to NEXT and send NEXT orders to Shopify for fulfillment"
---

Shop Sync connects a Shopify store with NEXT. Products managed in Shopify sync to the NEXT catalogue, and orders placed through NEXT can be sent to Shopify for fulfillment. Fulfillment updates then return to NEXT.

Shop Sync can also carry product customization details and selected order metadata to Shopify. Merchants can choose to send order confirmation emails from Shopify or NEXT to avoid duplicates.

To discuss whether Shop Sync fits your store, or to get setup and troubleshooting guidance, contact NEXT support or your NEXT account manager.
