---
title: "Apps and integrations"
description: "Find setup guides for NEXT apps and connected services"
---

Open **Apps** in your NEXT dashboard to find and manage installable apps. Use the guides below to configure the services your store needs. Tax services are configured under **Settings > Taxes** instead.

## Campaigns

- [**Campaigns App**](/docs/apps/campaigns-app) — Configure campaign domains, packages, and analytics. New stores include it by default.

## Fulfillment and delivery

- [**Shop Sync**](/docs/apps/shop-sync) — Sync Shopify products to NEXT and send NEXT orders to Shopify for fulfillment.
- [**ShipStation**](/docs/apps/shipstation) and [**3PL Central**](/docs/apps/3pl-central) — Connect fulfillment services.
- [**Delivery Tracking**](/docs/apps/delivery-tracking) — View carrier events and delivery reports. NEXT Payments stores receive it when their gateway is created.

## Marketing and support

- [**Klaviyo**](/docs/apps/klaviyo) — Send commerce events and sync products for email and SMS marketing.
- [**Meta Pixel**](/docs/apps/meta-pixel) and [**Everflow**](/docs/apps/everflow) — Connect advertising and partner attribution.
- [**Gorgias**](/docs/apps/gorgias) — Bring customer and order context into support tickets.

## Disputes

- [**Chargeback360**](/docs/apps/midigator) — Connect a dispute service. See the [Disputes Guide](/docs/features/payments/disputes-guide) for how dispute apps work with NEXT.

## Tax services

Choose a service by region under [**Settings > Taxes**](/docs/build-a-store/catalogue/tax-settings). [**Avalara AvaTax**](/docs/apps/avalara-avatax) and [**TaxJar**](/docs/apps/taxjar) have separate setup guides; neither is installed from the Apps menu.
