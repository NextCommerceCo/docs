Checkout.com is available as a bankcard gateway.

## Setup

* From **Settings > Payments**, choose **Add Gateway**
* Select **Checkout.com**
* Enter your credentials

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Client Id | Your Checkout.com client ID |
| Client Secret | The secret issued for that client ID |

## Payment Methods

Cards accepted: Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover, and JCB.

* [Apple Pay](/docs/features/payments/apple-pay)
* [Google Pay](/docs/features/payments/google-pay)

Alternative payment methods are not available on Checkout.com.

## 3DS2

[3DS2](/docs/features/payments/3ds2-payments) is supported. On the gateway's **3DS** card, choose **Enable 3D Secure Transactions**.

You then set up a **3DS Merchant Profile**. It needs your merchant name and the country your business is registered in, plus an **Acquirer BIN**, **Merchant ID (MID)**, and **MCC code** for each card network you want authenticated. Your acquiring bank or processing provider issues these values.

See [3DS2 Payments](/docs/features/payments/3ds2-payments) for Optimized 3DS2 and retrying cards that are not enrolled.

## Statement Descriptor

Checkout.com does not expose a statement descriptor setting on the gateway. The descriptor comes from your Checkout.com account.

## Authorize & Capture

Authorizations expire after 7 days. Refunds are issued as refunds, not voids. See [Authorize & Capture](/docs/features/payments/authorize-and-capture-payments).
