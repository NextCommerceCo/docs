Set up the countries you sell to in **Settings > Markets**. Each market has a currency and default language, with settings for pricing, payments, taxes, shipping, and fulfillment.

## Set Up Markets

1. Go to **Settings > Markets** and choose **Add Market**.
2. Select the country, currency, and default language. Review automatic pricing and rounding, then save.
3. Open the market to configure **Languages**, **Products & pricing**, **Payments**, and **Shipping & Fulfillment**.
4. Choose **Configure** beside **Taxes** to set up [tax collection](/docs/build-a-store/catalogue/tax-settings).
5. Check pricing and checkout for the market. Use **Deactivate** to keep it unavailable on the storefront until ready, then **Activate** to enable it.

Reorder markets to set their order in the storefront's market selector.

<Callout type="info">

Activating a market does not configure taxes. Tax services are assigned by country in **Settings > Taxes**. Built-in NEXT Tax's inclusive pricing setting applies by currency across all markets using that currency.

</Callout>

### Additional Languages

Open a market and choose **Manage** beside **Languages** to add languages and set their storefront order. Add translated content through the language tabs in your content editors.

The NEXT [Checkout](/docs/start-here/get-started/checkout-settings-and-policies) and customer [Notifications](/docs/start-here/get-started/support-and-notifications#notifications) are currently localized in a number of languages - contact your account representative if you have a custom language requirement.

## Currencies

Choose a currency when creating a market. Multiple markets can use the same currency.

Open the market and choose **Manage** beside **Products & pricing** to review its pricing settings. Confirm that products and shipping methods have prices in the market's currency before selling.

### Automatic Currency Pricing

Enable automatic forex pricing to create local prices using current exchange rates. Set a price-rounding rule to control the resulting prices. These rules also apply to new products imported through the API.

Review existing local prices before enabling automatic pricing.

<Callout type="info">

**Note:** For reporting purposes, transactions in all other currencies are converted to the base reporting currency in the Dashboard: **USD**

NEXT updates foreign exchange rates automatically, at the averaged daily rate via [Open Exchange Rates](https://openexchangerates.org/), to calculate foreign exchange reporting conversions.

</Callout>
